What it actually does, in plain English

You fund a BloFin account

Open a BloFin account, buy crypto with your debit card on Coinbase, send it to BloFin.

2

You create a BloFin API key

An API key lets the app place trades on your behalf. The How-To tab shows exactly which boxes to tick.

3

The app installs the assistant

Click Install Hermes. The app downloads the assistant, writes your keys into its sandbox, and registers a scheduled task.

You can hit Stop in the app, close it, or uninstall it. The bot stops within seconds. Your money never leaves BloFin.

Questions beginners actually ask

Does this work on a Mac?

Yes. KnightTrader BloFin ships a signed .dmg for macOS 11 (Big Sur) and newer on both Intel and Apple Silicon.

I have never bought crypto before. Can I still use this?

Yes. That is exactly who this is built for. The How-To tab walks you through every step.

I only have a debit card. Can I still fund this?

Yes. Buy USDC on Coinbase with your debit card, then withdraw it to your BloFin account.

Do I need a VPN?

Maybe. BloFin restricts some jurisdictions. The How-To tab includes a free ProtonVPN routine if you need one.

Do I have to place trades myself?

No. After setup, the assistant places and closes trades on schedule. You can stop the bot from the app anytime.

Where do my API keys go?

They stay on your machine. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Is my money ever in your hands?

No. Your USDC sits in your own BloFin account. The app only holds API keys.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel from your Stripe dashboard. You keep the app until the end of the billing period. Your funds on BloFin are untouched.

What happens if my payment fails?

The app shows a renewal banner and stops opening the Trading tab until you fix the payment.

Can I lose money?

Yes. KnightTrader BloFin trades perpetual futures. Only fund your BloFin account with money you can afford to lose in full.
